# Helping Lighthouse understand your language

You do not need to code to do this.

Lighthouse spots harmful messages by matching word lists, one per language and topic. The app is only as good as those lists. If you speak Nigerian Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa or Igbo, your knowledge of local slang is the most valuable thing you can give. This guide shows how to add and improve words from the Lighthouse admin, on your phone or computer.

## The big idea

- The word lists live on a server, not inside the app. When you publish, phones pick up the new words on their next check in. No app update.
- Nothing goes live until you publish. Everything is reversible.

## Signing in

1. Open the admin link and enter your email.
2. You get a sign in link by email. Tap it and you are in. No password.

## What a word is

Each entry is:

- the word or phrase, for example `sporty`, `i don stake my school fees`, `ma lu e`
- the language: English, Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa or Igbo
- the category: one of the eight topics below
- the severity: how serious it is

The eight categories: Violence, Sexual Content, Self Harm, Eating Disorders, Substance Use, Hate Speech, Gambling, Graphic Content.

Severity drives whether a parent gets alarmed, so be honest:

| Severity | Use it when | Example |
|---|---|---|
| high | real danger, explicit, or someone actively trying to do harm | "send nudes", "i wan die" |
| review | worth a parent's attention, not an emergency | "drop your betting code" |
| low | mild or borderline | "lottery" |

## Your day to day

1. Word list. Browse the live words by category and language. Search before adding, the word may already be there. Fix or remove anything wrong.
2. Suggestions. Words that Gemini proposed land here. They are never live until you approve them. For each one: approve, edit, or reject. Gemini is often wrong about Nigerian slang. Your check is the whole point.
3. Add your own. The real street slang Gemini would never know. This is where you matter most.
4. Publish. This creates a new version. Phones update on their next check in.

## Golden rules

- Every word a person approves. The system enforces this. Nothing automatic ever goes live.
- Quality beats quantity. A wrong word causes a false alarm and parents stop trusting alerts. A short correct list beats a long sloppy one.
- Only add words that are truly about the topic. Everyday words like "smoke" or "diet" cause false alarms. Leave them out.
- Watch ambiguous words. "igbo" can mean weed or the language. Do not add those as plain words. Tell the team so they can add a context rule.
- Only edit languages you actually speak. Guessing hurts children in that community.

## Why this matters

A child typing "I don stake my school fees for sporty" or "mo fe pa ara mi" is in real trouble, and only gets help if those words are on the list. You are the one who knows them. Thank you.
